## How cause funds work

Instead of choosing one family, you give to a cause. Every month our case team uses the fund to help verified campaigns in this category that are close to their goal or facing an urgent deadline.

- Every rupee goes to verified families in this category
- You receive an 80G receipt for your donation
- We publish a summary of how the fund was used in our annual report
