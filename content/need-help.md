## Who we can help

We support individuals and families who:

- Are facing a medical, education, housing or living-cost emergency
- Are not fully covered by government schemes or insurance
- Live in Delhi NCR (we are expanding to other cities)

## What happens next

1. Our case team will call you within **3 working days**
2. A case worker will visit and check your documents
3. If eligible, we will help you share your story with donors — only with your consent

All information you share is kept confidential.
