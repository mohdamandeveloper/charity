Our annual Walk for Hope returns on **Sunday, 14 February 2027**. Last year 1,600 walkers raised ₹18 lakh for families in our care.

## How to take part

1. Register as an individual, family or corporate team
2. Set up your fundraising page and share it with friends
3. Join us on the day for the walk, music and breakfast

Registration is free. Every walker receives a T-shirt and a medal.
