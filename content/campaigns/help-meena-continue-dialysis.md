Meena was diagnosed with kidney failure in March. Since then she has spent three mornings every week at a dialysis centre across the city.

Her husband Raju works as a painter on daily wages. On dialysis days he takes her to the hospital, which means losing a day's work. The family's savings ran out in August.

## What your donation will cover

- Six months of dialysis sessions (78 sessions)
- Auto-rickshaw travel to and from the hospital
- Medicines prescribed by her nephrologist

> "I just want to be well enough to see my daughter finish school." — Meena

Our case worker has met the family, checked hospital bills and confirmed the treatment plan with the hospital's social work department.
