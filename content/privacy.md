## Information we collect

When you donate, volunteer or contact us, we collect your name, email, phone number and (for 80G receipts) your PAN.

## How we use it

We use your information to process donations, issue receipts, reply to your messages and send updates you have agreed to receive. We never sell your data.

## Families we support

Stories are published only with written consent. Personal details are shared with donors only as far as the family has agreed.

## Contact

Email us any time to see, correct or delete your information.
