Brightpath Foundation was started in 2014 by a group of hospital social workers who kept meeting families with nowhere left to turn. A sudden illness, an accident at work or the loss of a job could push a family into debt within weeks, and government schemes often took months to help.

## How it works

1. **Referral** — A family contacts us directly, or a hospital, school or partner refers them.
2. **Verification** — Our case workers visit the family, check documents and confirm the need with hospitals, schools or landlords.
3. **Campaign** — With the family's consent, we publish their story with a clear goal and deadline.
4. **Payment** — Funds are paid directly to hospitals, schools or landlords wherever possible.
5. **Follow-up** — We stay in touch and share updates with donors.

## Our values

- **Dignity** — We share stories with consent and respect.
- **Transparency** — Our accounts are audited and published every year.
- **Community** — We bring donors, volunteers and families together.
