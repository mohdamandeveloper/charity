We work with companies, hospitals, schools and community groups to reach more families.

## How companies partner with us

- **CSR funding** — Fund a cause, a programme or our case team. We are eligible to receive CSR funds and report on impact.
- **Employee volunteering** — Team days at our Skills Club, sports meets or festival meals.
- **Payroll giving** — Let employees give a small amount every month.
- **In-kind support** — Medical equipment, laptops, groceries or professional services.

Tell us a little about your organisation and we will be in touch.
