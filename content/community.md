## Saturday Skills Club

Every Saturday, young adults with special needs join volunteers from our corporate partners for skills training in cooking, computers and customer service.

## Sports for All

Once a month we organise football and cricket meets where students, migrant workers, volunteers and former beneficiaries play together.

## Festival Meals

During Diwali, Eid and Christmas, we host shared meals that bring together families in our care with volunteers and neighbours.
