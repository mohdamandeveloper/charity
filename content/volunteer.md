Our 300 volunteers visit families, run events, mentor young people and help with photography, writing and design.

## Ways to volunteer

- **Case support** — Join our case workers on home visits (training provided)
- **Events** — Help run Walk for Hope, festival meals and sports meets
- **Mentoring** — Teach or mentor young people in our Skills Club
- **Skills** — Photography, writing, design or accounting

Fill in the form and our volunteer coordinator will contact you within a week.
