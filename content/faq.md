---
title: Frequently Asked Questions
eyebrow: Help
subtitle: Answers to common questions from donors and families.
faqs:
  - question: How do you verify campaigns?
    answer: Our case workers visit every family, check identity and income documents, and confirm costs directly with hospitals, schools or landlords before a campaign is published.
  - question: Will I get an 80G tax receipt?
    answer: Yes. Online donations receive an 80G receipt by email automatically. For UPI or bank transfers, email us your PAN and transaction ID.
  - question: How much of my donation reaches the family?
    answer: 100% of campaign donations go to the family. Only payment-gateway fees, shown at checkout, are deducted. Our running costs are covered by separate donations to Brightpath.
  - question: What happens if a campaign does not reach its goal?
    answer: The family receives everything that was raised. Our cause funds may top up campaigns that are close to their goal.
  - question: What happens if a campaign raises more than its goal?
    answer: Extra funds go to the cause fund for the same category and help other verified families.
  - question: Can I donate from outside India?
    answer: We can currently only accept donations from Indian bank accounts and cards. We are working on accepting international donations.
